### The Problem

**Buying a car in Nigeria is harder than it should be.**

You know the drill. You hear about a "clean" tokunbo (imported used car) from somebody who knows somebody. You make the trip to the market — maybe Berger, maybe Trade Fair. You spend the whole day there. The sun is beating. The pressure is real. "Oga, this is last price." You're not sure. The car looks fine on the outside, but what's going on underneath?

Then the questions start. Is this really a Belgian (European-spec vehicle), or did it come through Cotonou with a resprayed body? Is the mileage genuine? Why does the AC blow hot after five minutes? Did this car survive a flood somewhere in America? You want to ask, but you don't even know the right questions.

**And that's if you're lucky enough to find a car at all.** Many people visit markets three, four, five times and come back empty-handed. The prices online? They're never the final prices. The photos? Half of them are stock images. The "dealers"? Some of them are just middlemen adding their own cut on top.

It's exhausting. It's stressful. And worst of all — it's risky. Nigerians lose millions of naira every year to bad deals, hidden defects, and outright scams. This is not how buying a car should work.

---

### Our Story — Why Mottars Exists

**We didn't start Mottars because we saw a "market opportunity." We started it because we were tired of the same nonsense.**

Tired of the anxiety that comes with handing over your hard-earned money and hoping the car doesn't break down next week. Tired of scrolling through classifieds full of fake listings. Tired of a car market that punishes honest buyers and rewards dishonest sellers.

Founded in 2024 in Victoria Island, Lagos, Mottars was built on a simple belief: **every Nigerian deserves a trustworthy car buying experience.** Not a complicated one. Not a stressful one. A straightforward one — where you know the dealer is real, the car is what they say it is, and the price is the price.

**Here's what we do differently:**

- **Verified Dealers** — Every dealer on Mottars goes through a verification process. We check business registration, physical location, and track record. No anonymous sellers. No ghost listings.
- **Vehicle History Reports** — Know what you're buying before you buy it. Accident history, ownership records, the full picture.
- **Split Payments** — Not everyone can pay ₦5,000,000 upfront. Our partner dealers offer flexible payment plans so you can pay small-small and still drive a quality car.
- **Request-a-Car** — Can't find exactly what you want? Tell us what you're looking for. Verified dealers compete to find it for you. You describe it, they deliver it.

We're not trying to replace the car market. We're trying to fix what's broken about it.

### How It Works

**For Buyers:**

1. **Browse** — Search verified listings by make, model, price, and location. What you see is what's available.
2. **Verify** — Check vehicle history reports. Read dealer reviews. Ask questions directly.
3. **Buy with Confidence** — Make an offer, arrange a viewing, or split payments. Every transaction backed by a verified dealer.

**For Sellers & Dealers:**

1. **List Your Vehicle** — Create a detailed listing in minutes. Photos, specs, pricing — all in one place.
2. **Get Verified** — Complete our dealer verification process and earn a trust badge that buyers actually look for.
3. **Connect with Real Buyers** — No tyre-kickers. No time-wasters. Mottars brings you serious buyers who are ready to move.

### Our Values

**Trust First**
Everything we build starts with one question: does this make the buyer feel safer? If it doesn't, we don't ship it. Our verification process, vehicle history reports, and dealer reviews all exist for one reason — to earn your trust.

**Radical Transparency**
No hidden fees. No inflated prices. No bait-and-switch. The price you see is the price you discuss. The car you see is the car that exists. We believe transparency is the fastest way to build a market that works for everyone.

**Built for Real Life**
Not everyone has ₦10,000,000 in their account today. Not everyone lives five minutes from a car market. We build features like split payments and request-a-car because we design for how Nigerians actually live — not how a textbook says they should.

**Community Over Competition**
Mottars is a marketplace, not a dealership. We succeed when our dealers succeed, when our buyers find what they need, and when trust grows across the entire ecosystem. We're building this together.
