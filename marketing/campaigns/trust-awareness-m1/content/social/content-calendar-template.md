## 1. Platform Strategy Overview

## 3. Weekly Content Map

### Optimal Posting Times for Nigerian Audience

**Research basis**: Nigerian social media engagement peaks align with commute times, lunch breaks, and late evening scrolling patterns (WAT / GMT+1).

| Time Slot | Window (WAT) | Why It Works |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Morning Commute | 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM | Scrolling on the way to work, checking phones first thing |
| Lunch Break | 12:00 PM - 1:30 PM | Mid-day engagement spike, office workers on break |
| Evening Wind-down | 7:00 PM - 9:30 PM | Highest engagement window. People are home, relaxed, scrolling deeply |
| Late Night | 10:00 PM - 11:30 PM | Secondary spike, especially Twitter/X and TikTok |

**Platform-specific best times**:
- **Instagram**: 12:00 PM and 7:00 PM WAT (feed posts); Stories can go anytime between 8 AM - 10 PM
- **Twitter/X**: 8:00 AM, 12:30 PM, and 9:00 PM WAT (three daily windows)
- **TikTok**: 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM WAT (evening dominates for video discovery)

## 4. Content Format Templates

### Template 1: Instagram Carousel (Educational)

**Specs**: 1080 x 1080px, 7-10 slides, brand colors (#00C853, #1A1A2E, #FFFFFF)

| Slide | Purpose | Content Guide |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Slide 1 (Hook)** | Stop the scroll | Bold headline, large text, curiosity gap. Example: "5 Things Your Mechanic Won't Tell You" |
| **Slide 2 (Context)** | Set up the problem | Why this matters to the reader. 2-3 sentences max. |
| **Slides 3-8 (Value)** | One point per slide | One tip/fact/step per slide. Short text + supporting visual or icon. |
| **Slide 9 (Summary)** | Recap key points | Quick bullet list of all points covered. |
| **Slide 10 (CTA)** | Drive action | "Save this for later", "Share with someone buying a car", "Visit mottars.com" |

**Caption structure**:
```
[Hook line - must work before the "...more" cutoff]

[2-3 sentences expanding on the topic]

[CTA: Save, share, comment, or visit link in bio]

[Hashtags: 20-30, mix of tiers - see Hashtag Strategy]
```

### Template 2: Reel / TikTok Script

**Specs**: 1080 x 1920px (9:16), 30-60 seconds

```
HOOK (0-3 seconds):
[Opening line that creates curiosity or shock]
Example: "This one trick saved me ₦500,000 when buying my tokunbo..."

PROBLEM (3-8 seconds):
[State the pain point the audience relates to]
Example: "Most people walk into Berger with no plan and leave with regret."

SOLUTION / VALUE (8-45 seconds):
[Deliver the actual advice, tip, or story — 3-5 points max]
Example: Walk through the 3 checks to do before paying.

CTA (45-60 seconds):
[Tell them what to do next]
Example: "Follow @mottars for more car tips. Link in bio to find verified dealers."
```

**Text overlay rules**:
- Large, bold text matching spoken words
- Use Mottars Green (#00C853) for emphasis
- Keep text in the center-safe zone (avoid edges)
- Add captions — most people watch on mute

### Template 3: Instagram Story Series (Educational Arc)

**Specs**: 1080 x 1920px (9:16), 3-7 slides per series

| Slide | Type | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Slide 1** | Hook slide | Bold question or statement. Example: "How do you know if a car has been repainted?" |
| **Slide 2** | Context | Brief explanation of why this matters. |
| **Slides 3-5** | Tips/Steps | One tip per slide. Short text + visual demo if possible. |
| **Slide 6** | Interactive | Poll sticker ("Have you ever checked this?") or Question sticker ("What's your biggest car-buying fear?") |
| **Slide 7** | CTA | "Swipe up" / "Link in bio" / "DM us your question" |

**Design notes**:
- Use brand colors consistently
- Text should be readable without tapping
- Add location stickers (Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan) for reach
- Use countdown stickers for upcoming features/launches

### Template 4: Twitter/X Thread

**Specs**: 5-8 tweets, 240 characters max each

```
TWEET 1 (Hook):
[Bold claim, question, or hot take that makes people click "Show this thread"]
Example: "Nobody is talking about why tokunbo prices dropped 15% this month. Here's what's actually happening:"

TWEET 2-6 (Value):
[One point per tweet. Number them for clarity.]
Example:
"1/ The Naira strengthened against the dollar by 8% in February..."
"2/ Customs duty adjustments mean..."
"3/ This affects you because..."

TWEET 7 (Summary):
[Quick recap of what was covered]
"TL;DR: [key takeaway in one sentence]"

TWEET 8 (CTA):
[Drive action + ask for engagement]
"If this was useful, retweet Tweet 1 so other car buyers see it. Follow @mottars for more market intel."
```

### Template 5: Quote Card (Customer Testimonial)

**Specs**: 1080 x 1080px (Instagram) / 1200 x 675px (Twitter)

**Design elements**:
- Background: Mottars Green (#00C853) or Navy (#1A1A2E)
- Quote text: White, Inter Bold, large and centered
- Customer name: Below quote, Inter Regular, smaller
- Mottars logo: Bottom-right corner, small
- Optional: Customer photo (circular, with border)

**Copy format**:
```
"[Customer quote - 1-3 sentences, in their own words]"

— [First Name], [City]
[Context: e.g., "Bought a 2020 Honda Accord through Mottars"]

Caption: [Expand on the story briefly, end with CTA]
```

## 5. Recurring Series (Branded Content Series)

### Series 1: "Dealer Spotlight Tuesday"
- **Platform**: Twitter/X (thread) + Instagram (carousel or single post)
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Tuesday)
- **Format**: Thread (Twitter) / Carousel (Instagram)
- **Description**: Spotlight a verified Mottars dealer each week. Cover their story, inventory highlights, what makes them trustworthy, and a direct link to their Mottars profile. Builds trust by showing the humans behind the platform.
- **Hashtag**: #DealerSpotlight #MottarsVerified
- **Content structure**: Dealer photo, 3-5 facts about them, featured listing, CTA to visit their profile

### Series 2: "Tip of the Week"
- **Platform**: Instagram (carousel) + TikTok (video)
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Monday)
- **Format**: Carousel (7-10 slides) + Short video (30-45s)
- **Description**: One actionable car-buying, selling, or maintenance tip delivered in a visually engaging, save-worthy format. Designed for bookmarks and shares. Builds Mottars' authority as the go-to knowledge source.
- **Hashtag**: #MottarsTips #CarBuyingNG
- **Content structure**: Hook slide, problem context, tip explained in 5-7 steps, summary, CTA

### Series 3: "Scam Alert"
- **Platform**: Instagram (Reel) + Twitter/X (thread) + TikTok (video)
- **Frequency**: Bi-weekly (every other Thursday)
- **Format**: Reel/TikTok (45-60s) + Thread (6-8 tweets)
- **Description**: Expose a common car-buying scam with a real scenario walkthrough. Explain how to spot it, what to do if it happens, and how Mottars' verification prevents it. Highly shareable, positions Mottars as the protective authority.
- **Hashtag**: #ScamAlertNG #MottarsProtects
- **Content structure**: "This is how it works" (scam), "Here's what to look for" (red flags), "Here's how Mottars prevents this" (solution)

### Series 4: "Market Watch"
- **Platform**: Twitter/X (thread) + Instagram (Story series)
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Wednesday or Friday)
- **Format**: Thread (5-6 tweets) + Story series (4-5 slides)
- **Description**: Weekly snapshot of the Nigerian car market — price trends, popular models, customs duty updates, import news. Positions Mottars as the market intelligence source. Attracts both buyers and dealers.
- **Hashtag**: #MarketWatchNG #NaijaCarMarket
- **Content structure**: Key stat, what changed this week, what it means for buyers/sellers, prediction, CTA

### Series 5: "Buyer Story"
- **Platform**: Instagram (carousel or Reel) + Twitter/X (thread)
- **Frequency**: Bi-weekly (every other Saturday)
- **Format**: Carousel (5-7 slides with photos) or Reel (testimonial video) + Thread
- **Description**: Real stories from real Mottars buyers — their car-buying journey, challenges, and how Mottars helped. User-generated content when possible. Builds trust through social proof and relatable narratives.
- **Hashtag**: #MottarsStories #MyMottarsCar
- **Content structure**: The buyer's challenge, how they found Mottars, the car they bought, their experience, their advice to others

### Series 6: "Weekend Whip Watch"
- **Platform**: Instagram (Reel) + TikTok
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Friday)
- **Format**: Reel/TikTok (30-45s)
- **Description**: Fun, lighter content — showcase a beautiful car from Mottars listings, car culture moments, or "which would you pick?" comparisons. Weekend vibes, aspirational but accessible. Designed for shares and saves.
- **Hashtag**: #WeekendWhip #MottarsCars
- **Content structure**: Cinematic car reveal, key specs overlay, price, CTA to browse listings

### Series 7: "Ask Mottars"
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories + Twitter/X
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Thursday)
- **Format**: Story Q&A (question sticker) + Tweet thread answering top questions
- **Description**: Audience submits car-buying questions via IG Story question sticker. Best questions answered publicly in a follow-up Story arc or Twitter thread. Builds community, drives engagement, and creates content from audience input.
- **Hashtag**: #AskMottars
- **Content structure**: Question sticker post, select 3-5 best questions, answer each in a dedicated slide/tweet

## 6. Engagement Strategy

### Response Time Targets

| Channel | Target Response Time | Priority |
|---------|---------------------|----------|
| Instagram DMs | Within 2 hours (business hours: 8 AM - 9 PM WAT) | High |
| Instagram Comments | Within 4 hours | Medium |
| Twitter/X Mentions | Within 1 hour | High (public visibility) |
| Twitter/X DMs | Within 2 hours | Medium |
| TikTok Comments | Within 6 hours | Medium |

**After hours (9 PM - 8 AM)**: Respond within 2 hours of next business day start.

### Comment Engagement Rules

**Always respond to**:
- Direct questions about Mottars, cars, or services
- Negative feedback or complaints (escalate to CMO if serious)
- Genuine compliments or testimonials (amplify with a warm reply)
- Potential buyer signals ("How much?", "Is this still available?", "Where are you located?")

**How to respond**:
- Be warm, helpful, and human. Never robotic.
- Address the person by name when possible.
- Answer the question directly, then add a CTA if natural.
- For pricing questions: Direct them to the listing on mottars.com. Never quote prices in comments (they change).
- For complaints: Acknowledge, apologize if warranted, take to DMs for resolution. Never argue publicly.
- For trolls: Ignore or respond with light humor. Never engage seriously. Block repeat offenders.

**Engagement boosters**:
- Like every genuine comment within 4 hours.
- Reply to comments with a question to keep the thread going.
- Pin the best comment on each post (1 pinned comment per post).
- Respond to competitor audience comments on trending topics (don't attack competitors — add value).

### DM Handling Protocol

| DM Type | Response | Escalation |
|---------|----------|------------|
| General inquiry | Answer directly, link to relevant mottars.com page | None |
| Pricing question | "Check the listing at [link]. Prices are updated in real-time." | None |
| Complaint | Acknowledge, apologize, ask for details, escalate to CMO | CMO if unresolved |
| Partnership request | "Thanks for reaching out! Let me connect you with our team." Forward to info@mottars.com | Kemi (Growth) |
| Dealer sign-up inquiry | Share dealer sign-up link, highlight benefits | Kemi (Growth) |
| Spam / irrelevant | Ignore. Block if repeated. | None |

### Community Building Tactics

1. **Proactive engagement**: Comment on posts from Nigerian car enthusiasts, mechanics, automotive pages — add genuine value, not spam. 10-15 comments/day on relevant accounts.
2. **Repost user content**: When customers tag @mottars, repost to Stories (with credit). Ask permission for feed reposts.
3. **Engagement pods (organic)**: Build relationships with 10-15 Nigerian automotive/lifestyle creators for natural cross-engagement.
4. **Community hashtag**: Encourage #MottarsStories for buyers to share their experiences. Feature the best submissions.
5. **Weekly polls/questions**: Use Instagram Story polls and Twitter polls to drive participation. Ask about car preferences, buying experiences, market opinions.
6. **Reply with value**: Every reply should add something useful — a tip, a link, an insight. Never just "Thanks!" or generic responses.
7. **Trend-jacking**: When a relevant topic trends on Nigerian Twitter (car accidents, fuel prices, new car launches), join the conversation with Mottars' perspective. Must be genuine and helpful, never opportunistic about tragedies.

## Appendix: Quick Reference

### Content Creation Checklist (Before Every Post)

- [ ] Does it match the brand voice? (Trustworthy, approachable, confident, local-first, empowering)
- [ ] Is the hook strong enough to stop the scroll?
- [ ] Does it fit the content mix ratio for this week?
- [ ] Are hashtags from the approved hashtag sets? (See hashtag-strategy.md)
- [ ] Is there a clear CTA?
- [ ] Does it use the correct dimensions? (See brand-style.md)
- [ ] Has it been proofread for Nigerian English consistency?
- [ ] Are prohibited words avoided? (cheap, pre-owned, auction, used car lot)
- [ ] Is it scheduled at the optimal time for the platform?
- [ ] Does it end with a path to action on Mottars?
