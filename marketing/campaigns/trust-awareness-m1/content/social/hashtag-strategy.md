## 3. Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram

**Number of hashtags**: 20-30 per post (algorithm rewards thorough tagging in the automotive niche)

**Placement strategy**:
- **First comment method (recommended)**: Post caption clean, then immediately add all hashtags as the first comment. Keeps the caption readable while maintaining full discoverability.
- **Caption method (alternative)**: Place hashtags after 5 line breaks at the end of caption. Use if first-comment method causes timing issues.

**Hashtag rotation**: Rotate 30-40% of hashtags between posts to avoid being flagged as spam. Keep brand hashtags (#Mottars, #MottarsNG) constant, rotate niche and broad tags.

**Reels hashtags**: Use 10-15 max on Reels (algorithm handles discoverability differently). Focus on Tier 2 and 3 for Reels.

**Stories hashtags**: 3-5 max. Use the hashtag sticker (not text) for discoverability. Hide behind other stickers or shrink if cluttering the visual.

### Twitter/X

**Number of hashtags**: 2-3 per tweet maximum (more than 3 reduces engagement on Twitter)

**Placement strategy**:
- Integrate naturally into the tweet text. Example: "Nigerian car buyers deserve better. That's why every dealer on Mottars goes through verification. #BuySmartNG"
- Never start a tweet with a hashtag (reduces visibility in feeds).
- For threads: hashtags only in Tweet 1 and the final CTA tweet. No hashtags in the middle tweets.

**Hashtag selection**: Prioritize campaign hashtags (#BuySmartNG, #MottarsVerified) and one niche tag (#NaijaCars or #TokunboCars). Skip broad tags — they get lost on Twitter.

### TikTok

**Number of hashtags**: 3-5 per video in the caption

**Placement strategy**:
- Place in the video caption/description.
- Always include 1 trending TikTok hashtag alongside brand/niche tags.
- Monitor trending sounds and hashtag challenges weekly — adapt content to ride trends when relevant.

**Trending hashtags to monitor**:
- #LearnOnTikTok (educational content gets boosted)
- #NaijaTikTok (Nigerian creator community)
- #CarTok (automotive TikTok community)
- Check weekly: Nigerian trending page for relevant challenges

## 4. Branded Hashtags — Launch Strategy

### Primary Brand Hashtag: #Mottars

**Purpose**: Master brand identifier. Every single post from @mottars should include this.

**Launch strategy**:
1. Include #Mottars on every post from Day 1. Non-negotiable.
2. Add to bio on all platforms: "Tag us #Mottars"
3. Use in all reply tweets and comments.
4. Goal: Build a searchable library of all Mottars content under one tag.
5. Track post count monthly — target 500+ posts with #Mottars within 6 months (including community usage).

### Campaign Hashtag: #BuySmartNG

**Purpose**: Trust & Awareness campaign anchor. Positions Mottars as the "smart buyer's choice."

**Launch strategy**:
1. Introduce in Week 1 of Trust & Awareness campaign across all platforms.
2. Pair with educational and trust content (Sets A and C).
3. Create a pinned tweet explaining what #BuySmartNG means: "Buying a car in Nigeria shouldn't be a gamble. #BuySmartNG is our movement to help every buyer make informed, safe decisions."
4. Encourage audience to share their own car-buying tips with #BuySmartNG.
5. Feature the best community #BuySmartNG posts monthly.

### Trust Hashtag: #MottarsVerified

**Purpose**: Reinforce the verified dealer program as a core differentiator.

**Launch strategy**:
1. Use exclusively on trust and verification content (dealer spotlights, behind-the-scenes verification process, scam alerts).
2. Create an explainer post/thread: "What does #MottarsVerified actually mean? Here's our 5-step dealer verification process."
3. Encourage verified dealers to use #MottarsVerified on their own posts.
4. Goal: #MottarsVerified becomes synonymous with "trusted car dealer in Nigeria."

### Community Hashtag: #MottarsStories

**Purpose**: User-generated content engine. Buyers share their Mottars experience.

**Launch strategy**:
1. Soft launch after first 10-20 completed transactions.
2. DM buyers after purchase: "Congratulations on your new car! We'd love for you to share your experience. Post a photo with #MottarsStories and we might feature you!"
3. Feature 1 #MottarsStories post per week on the main account (with permission).
4. Create a highlight reel on Instagram dedicated to #MottarsStories.
5. Eventually run a monthly contest: "Best #MottarsStories post wins ___."

## 5. Competitor Hashtag Analysis

### Cars45

**Hashtags they use frequently**:
- #Cars45 (brand)
- #SellYourCar (core value prop)
- #CarValuation
- #NigeriaCars
- #LagosCars
- #UsedCars

**What works for them**: Strong brand hashtag adoption. #SellYourCar aligns perfectly with their sell-side focus.

**Gap**: No educational hashtags. No trust-building tags. No community engagement tags. Purely transactional.

**Mottars opportunity**: Own the educational and trust space that Cars45 ignores. They sell the transaction; we sell the confidence.

### Carlots.ng

**Hashtags they use frequently**:
- #Carlots (brand)
- #CarReview
- #CarPricing
- #NaijaCars
- #TokunboCars
- #CarDealerNigeria

**What works for them**: Car review and pricing content performs well. #CarPricing is a strong intent hashtag.

**Gap**: Zero trust content. No verification messaging. No scam awareness. No community-building hashtags. They review cars but never address the fear of buying them.

**Mottars opportunity**: Combine car knowledge (like Carlots) with trust messaging (which nobody does). Own #VerifiedDealer and #CarScamNigeria completely.

### Autochek

**Hashtags they use frequently**:
- #Autochek (brand)
- #CarFinancing
- #DriveNow
- #AfricaCars (pan-African positioning)
- #CarLoan

**What works for them**: Financing angle is strong. Pan-African positioning gives them scale perception.

**Gap**: Not localized enough for Nigerian buyers. No educational content hashtags. No community building. Feels corporate, not relatable.

**Mottars opportunity**: Out-localize them. Own the Nigerian-specific conversation. #NaijaCars, #TokunboCars, #LagosCars — these are the hashtags real Nigerian buyers search for. Autochek's pan-African play leaves the hyper-local lane open.

## 8. Quick Reference — Hashtag Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Always include #Mottars on every post across every platform
- Rotate 30-40% of non-brand hashtags between posts
- Use city-specific sets (Set F) for location-targeted content
- Check trending hashtags on Nigerian Twitter before posting
- Test new hashtags in batches and track results
- Place Instagram hashtags in the first comment for clean captions
- Integrate Twitter hashtags naturally into tweet copy

### Don't
- Use more than 3 hashtags on Twitter/X (kills engagement)
- Use banned or flagged Instagram hashtags (check regularly)
- Use the exact same hashtag set on every post (Instagram may shadowban)
- Use hashtags with zero posts — they have no discovery value
- Hashtag-stuff TikTok captions — keep to 3-5 max
- Use competitor brand names as hashtags (#Cars45, #Autochek) — looks desperate
- Start any tweet with a hashtag — it reduces visibility in feeds
- Use hashtags that attract the wrong audience (#luxury, #exotic, #supercar)
