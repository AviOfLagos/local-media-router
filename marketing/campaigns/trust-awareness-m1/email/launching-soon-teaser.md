## 2. Email Capture Mechanism

### Options Evaluation

| Option | Cost | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|--------|------|------|------|---------|
| **Google Form** | Free | Instant setup, no account needed, works on mobile | Ugly, no branding, no email automation, feels unprofessional, no analytics | Not recommended |
| **Brevo Landing Page** | Free (300 emails/day) | Built-in email marketing, branded pages, contact management, automation, GDPR-compliant | Requires account setup, slight learning curve, free plan limits design templates | **RECOMMENDED** |
| **Website Popup/Banner** | Free (dev time) | Best UX, stays on mottars.com domain, SEO benefit, highest trust | Requires developer, longer to ship, needs backend for email storage | Phase 2 (after validation) |
| **Linktree (Free)** | Free | Quick setup, link-in-bio standard | Very limited customization, no email integration, another intermediary page | Backup for Instagram only |

### Recommendation: Brevo Landing Page + Brevo Email List

**Why Brevo wins:**
1. **Free tier is generous:** 300 emails/day, unlimited contacts, landing page builder
2. **All-in-one:** Capture emails AND send welcome emails AND nurture sequences from one platform
3. **Branded:** Custom landing page with Mottars branding (not a generic Google Form)
4. **Automation built in:** Auto-send the Tokunbo Buyer's Guide on signup
5. **Analytics:** Track signups, open rates, click rates from day 1
6. **Scalable:** When we hit 1,000+ subscribers, upgrade path is clear

### Exact Setup Steps for Brevo Landing Page

**Pre-requisite:** CEO creates Brevo account at brevo.com (agents cannot create accounts).

**Step 1: Create Contact List**
1. Go to Contacts > Lists > Create a New List
2. Name: "Early Subscribers — Mottars Launch"
3. Create a second list: "Dealer Prospects" (for later use)

**Step 2: Build Landing Page**
1. Go to Campaigns > Landing Pages > Create Landing Page
2. Choose a single-column template (clean, mobile-first)
3. Page elements:
   - **Header:** Mottars logo
   - **Headline:** "Get Nigeria's First Free Tokunbo Buyer's Guide"
   - **Subheadline:** "Plus: Be the first to know when verified cars drop in your city"
   - **Hero image:** Clean car photo or Mottars branded graphic
   - **3 Bullet points:**
     - "Learn exactly what to check before you pay"
     - "Get notified when new verified listings drop in Lagos, Abuja, or Ibadan"
     - "Join 100+ smart Nigerian car buyers (and counting)"
   - **Form fields:** First Name, Email, City (dropdown: Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Other)
   - **CTA button:** "Send Me the Free Guide" (button color: Mottars primary brand color)
   - **Trust line below button:** "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime. We respect your inbox."
   - **Footer:** Mottars logo, website link, copyright
4. Set form to add contacts to "Early Subscribers — Mottars Launch" list
5. Set confirmation page text: "You're in! Check your email for the Tokunbo Buyer's Guide."

**Step 3: Set Up Welcome Email Automation**
1. Go to Automations > Create New Automation
2. Trigger: "Contact added to list: Early Subscribers — Mottars Launch"
3. Action: Send email
4. Email content:
   - **Subject:** "Your free Tokunbo Buyer's Guide is here"
   - **From:** Mottars <info@mottars.com>
   - **Body:** Welcome message + link to download guide (PDF or link to blog post)
   - **CTA:** "Browse verified cars on Mottars" (link to mottars.com)

**Step 4: Get Landing Page URL**
1. Publish the landing page
2. Copy the URL (e.g., pages.brevo.com/mottars-guide)
3. Use this URL in all social media CTAs, link-in-bio, and QR codes

**Step 5: Connect to Social**
1. Add Brevo landing page URL to Instagram bio link
2. Add to Twitter/X bio
3. Use as the link in all teaser posts

### Placement 3: Exit Intent Popup

**Location:** Triggers when user moves cursor toward browser close/back button (desktop only)
**Type:** Modal popup with email capture
**Copy:**

> **Headline:** Wait — Before You Go
> **Body:** Get our free Tokunbo Buyer's Guide. 10-point inspection checklist, pricing guide, and negotiation tips used by professional dealers. Yours free.
> **Form:** [Email] [Button: "Send Me the Guide"]
> **Dismiss:** "No thanks, I'll figure it out myself" (light grey text link)

**Dev Spec:**
- Modal overlay (semi-transparent dark background)
- Centered card, max-width 500px
- Trigger: `mouseout` event on `document` when `clientY < 0` (cursor leaves viewport at top)
- Frequency: Show once per session (use `sessionStorage` flag)
- Cookie: Don't show again for 7 days after dismissal (use `localStorage` with timestamp)
- Mobile: Do NOT show exit intent popup (poor UX on mobile). Instead, show a bottom slide-up bar after 60 seconds on page
- Same Brevo API integration
- Close on ESC key, click outside, or X button

---

### Placement 4: Floating Bottom Bar (Mobile)

**Location:** Fixed bar at bottom of screen on mobile, appears after 30 seconds
**Type:** Slim banner with CTA
**Copy:**

> **Text:** "Get the free Tokunbo Buyer's Guide" [Button: "Get It"]

**Dev Spec:**
- Fixed position, bottom: 0, full-width, 60px height
- Slides up with CSS animation after 30-second delay
- Dismiss X button on right side
- Tapping "Get It" scrolls to homepage form or opens Brevo landing page in new tab
