**Key Finding**: The trust/verification content space is wide open. Only two small dealer-specific sites (carsalesmannigltd.com, hexoautos.ng) cover dealer lies/scams — both are local businesses in Asaba, not marketplaces. No major competitor owns this topic. Mottars can dominate it.

Carlots.ng is active but focused on car reviews and pricing — not trust content. The only live "dealer scam" content comes from sites with limited domain authority.

**Recommendation**: Execute blog #1 and #2 aggressively. The window is open, but Carlots.ng is publishing fast (15+ posts visible, 2026-focused). If they pivot to trust content, our first-mover advantage closes.

## Content Landscape for Blog #1: "9 Lies Car Dealers Tell"

### Existing Competition

| Article | Source | Domain Authority | Words | Quality | Threat |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|---------|--------|
| "Common Lies Car Buyers Hear in Nigeria (2026)" | carsalesmannigltd.com | Low (local dealer in Asaba) | ~1,300 | Medium — practical but promotional, Asaba-focused | LOW |
| "7 Secrets Nigeria Car Dealers Won't Tell You" | blog.hexoautos.ng | Low (small dealer blog) | ~700 | Low — truncated, thin | VERY LOW |
| "How to Avoid Car Scams in Nigeria" | dreamcar.ng | Medium | ~1,500 | Good — covers scam types broadly | MEDIUM |
| "What to Avoid When Buying Tokunbo" | 234drive.com | Medium-High | ~1,200 | Good — authoritative but general | MEDIUM |
| Nairaland scam threads | nairaland.com | Very High | Varies | Raw — unstructured user stories | HIGH (for search traffic) |

### Opportunity Analysis

**Key Finding**: No marketplace has published a definitive "lies dealers tell" article. Existing content comes from small dealers (self-serving) or general automotive blogs (not marketplace-backed). Nairaland threads get massive traffic but are unstructured.

**Evidence**:
- carsalesmannigltd.com covers 7 lies but is Asaba-focused, promotional, ~1,300 words
- hexoautos.ng is thin (~700 words) and poorly structured
- Dreamcar.ng is broader but doesn't position itself as a solution
- No article has schema markup for FAQ or HowTo
- No article includes a checklist or downloadable resource

**Recommendation for Chidi**: Target primary keyword "car dealer lies Nigeria" with secondary "car dealer scam Nigeria", "how to spot fake car dealer Nigeria". The Nairaland thread (5M+ views) proves massive search demand. Our article at 2000+ words with FAQ schema, 9 distinct lies (more than any competitor), and a Mottars verified-dealer CTA will dominate.

**Recommendation for Dayo**: Emotional arc should be: anger (these lies are real) -> recognition (I've heard these) -> empowerment (now I know) -> trust (Mottars prevents this). Don't be preachy — the carsalesmannigltd article reads like an ad. Ours should read like a friend warning you.

## Content Landscape for Blog #2: "Tokunbo Inspection Checklist"

### Existing Competition

| Article | Source | Domain Authority | Words | Quality | Threat |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|---------|--------|
| "How to Verify a Tokunbo Car Before Payment (2026)" | carsalesmannigltd.com | Low | ~950 | Medium — 7 steps, practical but thin | LOW |
| "Things to Check When Buying a Used Car in Nigeria" | sellatease.com | Low-Medium | ~1,000 | Medium — general, not tokunbo-specific | LOW |
| "Buying Used Cars in Nigeria: Tips That Actually Help" | naijacarlovers.com | Low | ~1,200 | Medium — practical tips, 2026 content | LOW |
| Various Nairaland threads | nairaland.com | Very High | Varies | Raw user experiences, unstructured | HIGH (traffic) |

### Opportunity Analysis

**Key Finding**: No comprehensive tokunbo inspection checklist exists from a trusted marketplace. The best existing article (carsalesmannigltd.com) is only ~950 words, covers 7 steps, and is Asaba-specific. Nobody provides a printable/downloadable checklist.

**Evidence**:
- carsalesmannigltd covers: VIN matching, documents, accident signs, flood detection, electronics, test drive, payment safety — good framework but thin execution
- Missing from ALL articles: specific warning light codes, sample document photos, inspection cost estimates, legal recourse, regional variations, mechanic recommendations
- Zero HowTo schema on any competing article
- Zero downloadable PDF or checklist format

**Recommendation for Chidi**: Target "tokunbo inspection checklist" as primary. This is literally an unclaimed featured snippet opportunity. HowTo schema + FAQ schema on this article. Secondary keywords: "how to check tokunbo car", "things to check before buying used car Nigeria", "tokunbo car inspection guide".

**Recommendation for Dayo**: This should be the article people screenshot and take to the car lot. Make it feel like a tool, not an article. Numbered steps, checkbox format, "what good looks like vs what bad looks like" framing. The carsalesmannigltd version is useful but reads like a blog post. Ours should read like the only checklist you'll ever need.

### For Dayo (Creative Briefs — TASK-D-002, TASK-D-003)

1. **Tone benchmark**: Carlots.ng writes authentic Nigerian English well. Our content must be equally culturally fluent but with a trust/safety angle they completely lack.
2. **Avoid**: The carsalesmannigltd.com tone — it reads like an ad for their dealership. Our tone should be "friend warning you" not "dealer selling to you."
3. **Emotional differentiation**: Nobody in the market makes the reader feel empowered. They either scare (scam articles) or sell (dealer content). Mottars should be the brand that gives you confidence.
4. **Campaign concept hook**: "What Nigerian car dealers don't want you to know" — curiosity-driven, positions Mottars as the insider giving away dealer secrets.

### For Tunde (Blog Writing — TASK-T-001, TASK-T-002)

1. **Word count target**: 2000-2500 words for both. Longest competitor article is 1,300 words. We need to be 2x deeper.
2. **Structure to beat**: carsalesmannigltd uses a clean 7-item structure with FAQ. We should do 9 items (more comprehensive) + FAQ + downloadable checklist.
3. **Language**: Use glossary terms naturally (tokunbo, Belgian, first body, Berger). Define on first use for SEO.
4. **Differentiation**: Every lie/tip should connect to a Mottars feature (verified dealers, vehicle history, request-a-car) — but subtly, not as hard sells.

### For Amara (Social Content — TASK-A-002, TASK-A-003)

1. **Format gap**: No competitor does educational carousels about trust/safety. This is completely open.
2. **Instagram approach**: "One lie per slide" carousel format for Blog #1. "One inspection step per slide" for Blog #2.
3. **Hashtag intel**: Competitors use basic hashtags (#cars, #nigeria). A trust-focused hashtag strategy (#MottarsVerified, #BuyCarSafely) would be unique.
4. **Reel format**: "POV: The dealer says 'nothing to fix'" — relatable scenario reels drive highest engagement in Nigerian IG.

### For Kemi (Outreach — TASK-K-001, TASK-K-002)

1. **Dealer angle**: Cars45 dealers are orphaned (platform acquired by Jiji, blog dead). Target former Cars45 verified dealers for outreach.
2. **Email opportunity**: First-mover advantage — no automotive marketplace in Nigeria does email marketing.
