## Outreach Strategy Notes

### Wave 1: Priority A Dealers (Week 1-2)
- **Target:** 9 dealers
- **Approach:** Personalized cold email (TASK-K-002 sequence) + Instagram DM follow-up
- **Value prop lead:** "Your cars deserve more buyers than just walk-in traffic"
- **Offer:** Free listing for first 5 vehicles, verified dealer badge, priority placement
- **Goal:** Onboard 3-5 dealers from this wave

### Wave 2: Priority B Dealers (Week 3-4)
- **Target:** 12 dealers
- **Approach:** Email sequence + reference Wave 1 success stories
- **Value prop lead:** "Join [Wave 1 dealer name] and other verified dealers on Mottars"
- **Goal:** Onboard 5-7 dealers from this wave

### Wave 3: Priority C Dealers (Month 2)
- **Target:** 4 dealers
- **Approach:** Broader outreach, potentially through WhatsApp or phone
- **Goal:** Onboard 2-3 dealers from this wave

### Key Talking Points for All Outreach
1. **Free to list** — no upfront cost, commission-based model
2. **Verified badge** — builds trust with buyers, reduces tire-kickers
3. **Request-a-car** — buyers come to YOU describing what they want
4. **Split payments** — reach buyers who can't pay full upfront
5. **Multi-city reach** — your cars visible to buyers in Lagos, Abuja, and Ibadan
6. **Vehicle history reports** — differentiate from unverified classifieds

### Competitor Angles
- **To former Cars45 dealers:** "Cars45 was acquired by Jiji. Their dealer program is dead. Mottars is the new home for serious dealers."
- **To Jiji-frustrated dealers:** "Tired of fake inquiries on Jiji? Mottars buyers are verified and serious."
- **To Instagram-only dealers:** "You're doing great content on Instagram. Now imagine all those cars also visible to thousands of buyers on a trusted marketplace."
