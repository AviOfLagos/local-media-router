## Brand Constraint Block (Inject into ALL AI image prompts)

```
Style: Clean, modern, trustworthy Nigerian automotive marketplace.
Colors: Primary green #00C853, dark navy #1A1A2E, white #FFFFFF.
Setting: Nigerian — Lagos, Abuja, real streets, real car lots, warm golden lighting.
People: Authentic Nigerian individuals, warm expressions, professional but approachable.
Cars: Mid-range vehicles (Toyota Camry, Honda Accord, Lexus RX), realistic conditions.
Mood: Confident, empowering, trustworthy. NOT luxury. NOT corporate. NOT Western.
Avoid: Cold blue tones, stock photo aesthetics, AI-generated faces, empty showrooms.
```

## 1. Instagram Profile Picture (320x320)

### Design Specification
- **Shape**: Circular display (Instagram crops square to circle)
- **Content**: Mottars wordmark "M" lettermark (the capital M from the Mottars logo), centered
- **Background**: Solid Mottars Green #00C853
- **Lettermark color**: White #FFFFFF
- **Style**: Clean, bold, instantly recognizable at small sizes. The M should take up about 60% of the circle's diameter
- **Border/padding**: The green background should extend fully to all edges -- Instagram adds its own circular crop and ring
- **File format**: PNG with no transparency (solid green background)

### AI Image Prompt (Midjourney)
```
A clean, minimal brand icon for a Nigerian automotive marketplace. A bold, geometric white capital letter "M" centered on a solid vibrant green (#00C853) background. The M is modern, slightly rounded, in a bold sans-serif style similar to Inter Bold. No gradients, no shadows, no additional elements. Square format, 320x320 pixels. Flat design, professional, app-icon style. --ar 1:1 --s 50 --no gradients shadows 3d text outlines
```

### AI Image Prompt (DALL-E)
```
A square brand icon, 320x320 pixels. Solid bright green background (hex #00C853). A large, bold, white capital letter "M" in a clean sans-serif font centered in the frame. The M takes up about 60% of the space. Flat design, no gradients, no shadows, no other elements. Modern, minimal, professional. Suitable for a social media profile picture.
```

### Canva Alternative
If generating via Canva:
1. Create 320x320 canvas
2. Background: #00C853
3. Add text "M" -- Inter Bold, White #FFFFFF, size 180px, centered both axes
4. Export as PNG

## 4. Twitter/X Header (1500x500)

### Design Specification

**Layout (left to right):**
- **Left third (0-500px)**: Mottars wordmark logo in white, vertically centered
- **Center third (500-1000px)**: Tagline text "Know Before You Go." in white, Inter Semi-bold 600, vertically centered. Below it in smaller text: "Nigeria's Trusted Car Marketplace" in white at 60% opacity
- **Right third (1000-1500px)**: Subtle background imagery -- abstract shapes or a faded car silhouette in slightly lighter navy (#16213E), adding visual interest without clutter

**Background**: Solid Navy Black #1A1A2E
**Accent**: A thin horizontal line in Mottars Green #00C853 running across the bottom 8px of the entire header (like a brand bar)
**Text color**: White #FFFFFF
**Overall feel**: Clean, authoritative, professional. Reads clearly on both mobile and desktop

### Safe Zone Notes
- Twitter/X profile picture overlaps the bottom-left of the header on desktop. Keep critical content (logo, tagline) in the center and right
- Mobile crops the top and bottom. Keep all text within the vertical center 300px (y: 100 to y: 400)

### AI Prompt (Midjourney)
```
A Twitter header banner, 1500x500 pixels, for a Nigerian automotive marketplace called Mottars. Dark navy background (#1A1A2E). On the left, the word "Mottars" in clean white sans-serif font. In the center, the tagline "Know Before You Go." in white semi-bold text. A thin bright green (#00C853) horizontal stripe runs along the very bottom edge. On the right side, a subtle, faded light silhouette of a sedan car in slightly lighter navy. Clean, modern, professional, minimal. No photos, no people. --ar 3:1 --s 50 --no photos people faces gradients
```

### AI Prompt (DALL-E)
```
A Twitter header image, 1500x500 pixels. Dark navy background (hex #1A1A2E). Left side: the word "Mottars" in clean white sans-serif font, large and bold. Center: the text "Know Before You Go." in white, slightly smaller. Right side: a subtle faded silhouette of a sedan car in slightly lighter navy (hex #16213E). A thin bright green stripe (hex #00C853, about 8 pixels tall) runs along the entire bottom edge. Clean, modern, minimal design. No photographs, no people.
```

### Canva Build Instructions
1. Create 1500x500 canvas
2. Background: #1A1A2E
3. Left: Add "Mottars" text -- Inter Bold, White #FFFFFF, ~48px
4. Center: Add "Know Before You Go." -- Inter Semi-bold, White #FFFFFF, ~32px
5. Below center text: "Nigeria's Trusted Car Marketplace" -- Inter Regular, White at 60% opacity, ~18px
6. Bottom: Rectangle 1500x8px, #00C853, snapped to bottom edge
7. Right: Optional -- search Canva for "car silhouette" element, color it #16213E, position at right, low opacity
8. Export as PNG or JPG
