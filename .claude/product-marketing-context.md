# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-05*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Nigeria's trusted automotive marketplace for buying, selling, and renting quality vehicles.
**What it does:** Mottars connects car buyers with verified dealers across Nigeria, offering vehicle sales (new and used), auto parts, and mechanic services through a centralized digital platform. It simplifies the traditionally opaque Nigerian car-buying experience with verified listings, split payment options, and vehicle history reports.
**Product category:** Automotive marketplace / online car dealership platform
**Product type:** Marketplace (two-sided: buyers and sellers/dealers)
**Business model:** Commission on vehicle transactions, dealer subscription plans, premium listing fees

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Individual car buyers/sellers, used car dealers, auto parts suppliers, mechanic workshops across Nigeria
**Decision-makers:** Individual buyers (direct), dealer owners/managers, fleet managers at SMEs
**Primary use case:** Finding and buying a reliable vehicle from a verified dealer without the stress of traditional car markets
**Jobs to be done:**
- Buy a quality vehicle from a trusted source at a fair price
- Sell a vehicle quickly to a large audience of ready buyers
- Find reliable mechanics and genuine auto parts nearby
**Use cases:**
- First-time car buyer searching for affordable, verified used cars
- Dealer listing inventory to reach more buyers beyond physical location
- Car owner looking for certified mechanics for repairs
- Buyer wanting to split payments across installments via partner dealers

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Buying and selling vehicles in Nigeria is stressful, risky, and opaque — buyers fear scams, sellers struggle to find serious buyers, and trust is extremely low across the market.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Jiji/OLX: Flooded with fake listings, no verification, high scam risk
- Cars45: Focused on buying cars from sellers (one-sided), limited selection for buyers
- Traditional car markets (Berger, Trade Fair): No transparency, intimidating negotiation, no recourse
**What it costs them:** Hours wasted on fake leads, money lost to scams or inflated pricing, anxiety about vehicle condition, missed opportunities from limited market reach
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being cheated, frustration with the process, desire for a "normal" car-buying experience like in developed markets

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Autochek — pan-African automotive marketplace, but premium-focused and less localized for the average Nigerian buyer
**Secondary:** Jiji Motors — classified listings platform, but zero verification means high scam risk
**Indirect:** Physical car markets (Berger Auto Market, Trade Fair) — established but stressful, opaque, and risky

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Verified dealer program with trust badges
- Split payment options through partner dealers
- Request-a-car feature (buyers describe what they want, dealers compete to fulfill)
- Vehicle history reports
- Multi-category (cars + parts + mechanics in one platform)
**How we do it differently:** We verify dealers before they list, provide vehicle history transparency, and let buyers request specific vehicles instead of just searching
**Why that's better:** Reduces scam risk dramatically, saves time, gives buyers negotiating power
**Why customers choose us:** Trust (verified dealers), convenience (request-a-car), and affordability (split payments)

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How do I know the dealer is legit?" | Every dealer goes through our verification process — we check business registration, physical location, and track record |
| "I prefer seeing cars in person at the market" | You can still visit the dealer — Mottars helps you find the right car and dealer first, saving you from wasted trips |
| "What if the car has hidden problems?" | We provide vehicle history reports and encourage independent inspections — verified dealers also offer limited guarantees |

**Anti-persona:** People who want brand-new luxury cars only (we focus on the affordable to mid-range market); people who enjoy the haggling culture of physical markets

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with scams on classifieds, wasted trips to markets, fear of buying a lemon
**Pull:** Verified dealers, split payments, request-a-car convenience, vehicle history reports
**Habit:** "I always buy from someone my friend knows" — referral-based trust is deeply ingrained
**Anxiety:** "What if Mottars dealers are also not trustworthy?" — verification process and reviews help overcome this

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem:**
- "You can't trust anybody selling cars in Lagos"
- "I went to Berger three times and still didn't find what I wanted"
- "The prices online are never the real prices"
- "I'm scared of buying a car with engine problems"
**How they describe us:**
- "They actually verify the dealers"
- "I found my car without stress"
- "You can even do installment payments"
**Words to use:** Verified, trusted, stress-free, genuine, affordable, flexible payments, tokunbo (imported used), Belgian (premium used), Naija, no wahala
**Words to avoid:** Cheap (implies low quality), used car lot, pre-owned (too formal/Western), auction
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tokunbo | Imported used vehicle (from "to ku n bo" — coming from abroad) |
| Belgian | Premium/well-maintained used vehicle, usually from Europe |
| Berger | Popular open-air car market in Lagos |
| Cotonou car | Vehicle imported through Benin Republic (sometimes with questionable documentation) |
| Custom duty | Import tax — a major cost factor for tokunbo vehicles |
| Installment/Split payment | Paying for a vehicle in multiple installments rather than full upfront |
