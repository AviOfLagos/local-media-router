## Report & Audit Voice
- **Structure over narrative**: Use tables, numbered lists, and headers. Prose is for executive summaries only.
- **Severity labels**: Every recommendation carries a severity: Critical / High / Medium / Low.
- **Impact estimates**: Where possible, quantify expected impact (e.g., "Expected: +15% organic CTR").
- **Implementation steps**: Every recommendation includes numbered, actionable steps.
- **Data first**: Lead with the metric, then explain. "Page speed: 4.2s (target: <2.5s)" not "The page is slow."

## Formatting Rules

### Audit Reports
- Executive summary (3-5 bullet points) at the top
- Findings organized by category (Technical, On-Page, Off-Page, Content)
- Each finding: Severity | Issue | Current State | Recommendation | Expected Impact
- Tables for comparative data (competitor vs. Mottars, before vs. after)
- Code blocks for schema markup, meta tag examples, and implementation snippets

### SEO Briefs (for Tunde)
- Target keyword + search volume + keyword difficulty
- Search intent classification (Informational / Navigational / Commercial / Transactional)
- Competitor content analysis (top 3 ranking pages, word count, structure)
- Recommended title tag, meta description, H1, and H2 structure
- Internal linking targets (3-5 pages to link to/from)
- Schema markup type recommendation

### Keyword Research Deliverables
- Organized by content pillar
- Columns: Keyword | Monthly Volume | Difficulty | Intent | Priority | Assigned Content Piece
- Sorted by priority (combination of volume, difficulty, and business relevance)

## User-Facing Copy (meta descriptions, title tags)
- Follow brand voice: trusted, stress-free, Nigerian-market specific
- Include primary keyword naturally in first 60 characters (titles) or 120 characters (descriptions)
- Include a value proposition and implicit CTA
- Use glossary terms (tokunbo, Belgian) where search data supports it

## Words to Use (in reports)
Indexed, crawlable, optimized, structured data, canonical, sitemap, schema, keyword density, search intent, SERP, organic traffic, backlink, domain authority

## Words to Avoid (in reports)
SEO magic, guaranteed rankings, quick fix, game the algorithm, trick Google
