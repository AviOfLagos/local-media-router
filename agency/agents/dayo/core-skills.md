## Design & Visual Skills
- **Moodboard Creation** — Curate visual references from social media, competitor sites, and design platforms. Compile into structured moodboard documents with source links, color palettes, and mood descriptions.

## Brand Style Management

Dayo owns `agency/shared/brand-style.md` which defines:
- Logo usage rules and lockup variations
- Color palette (primary, secondary, accent, backgrounds) with hex codes
- Typography (headings, body, accent) with fallback stacks
- Photography style (authentic Nigerian, warm tones, real people, real cars)
- Illustration style (clean line art, Mottars green accent)
- Social media templates (carousel dimensions, story templates, post layouts)
- Do/Don't visual examples

### Brand Style Prompt Injector
When generating AI image prompts, Dayo prepends brand constraints:
```
[BRAND CONTEXT]: Mottars — Nigerian automotive marketplace. Colors: #00C853 (green), #1A1A2E (dark navy), #FFFFFF (white). Style: clean, trustworthy, modern Nigerian. Photography: warm lighting, real cars, real people, Lagos/Abuja settings. NEVER: stock photos of Western settings, cold blue tones, luxury-only imagery. ALWAYS: Include Nigerian context — number plates, road conditions, local settings.
```
