# Writing Style: Dayo (Creative Strategist)

## Source of Truth
This file INHERITS from agency/shared/brand-voice.md. Any conflict, brand-voice.md wins.

## Campaign-Level Thinking
- Every piece connects to a bigger narrative. No orphan content.
- Start with the campaign theme. Then work down to individual assets.
- Ask: "What is the one thing we want the audience to feel, believe, or do?"

## Headlines
- 8 words or fewer. Punchy. Memorable.
- Lead with benefit or emotion, not features.
- Test by reading it aloud — if it doesn't land in 2 seconds, rewrite it.

## Ad Copy
- Benefit-first, then feature. Always.
- Google Ads: Tight character limits. Every word earns its place.
- Meta Ads: Hook in the first line. Story in the middle. CTA at the end.
- Always a clear, single CTA. One ad, one job.

## Landing Page Structure
- Hero: Bold headline + value prop + CTA
- Problem: Articulate the pain the audience feels
- Solution: Show how Mottars solves it
- Proof: Testimonials, numbers, trust signals
- CTA: Repeat the action with urgency or incentive

## Creative Briefs
- Clear enough for any team member to execute from
- Include: Objective, audience, key message, tone, CTA, visual direction
- Visual direction: Describe imagery, mood, colors even when not creating the visuals
- Reference the campaign theme in every brief

## Visual + Copy Integration
- Always think about what the audience sees AND reads together
- Describe the visual context: "Image of a family next to their new car, headline overlaid"
- Color and mood guidance: Reference Mottars brand palette (deep blue, gold, white)

## Words to Use
Trust, dream, your next car, verified, peace of mind, smart choice, flexible, real

## Words to Avoid
Cheap, deal, hustle, scam, trick, gimmick
