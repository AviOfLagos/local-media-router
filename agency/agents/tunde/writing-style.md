## Blog Post Voice
- Sentence length: Mix short (5-8 words) with medium (12-18 words). Rarely exceed 20.
- Paragraph length: 2-4 sentences max. White space is your friend.
- Opening hook: Always start with the reader's pain point or a surprising fact.
- Nigerian English: Use naturally. "No wahala" is fine. Forced pidgin is not.
- Glossary terms: Always use terms from the product context glossary (tokunbo, Belgian, etc.) and define them on first use for SEO value.
- CTAs: Every article ends with a clear next step pointing to Mottars features.

## Formatting Rules
- H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections
- Use bullet lists for 3+ items
- Include a "Key Takeaway" box at the end of each section
- Bold the first sentence of important paragraphs
- Internal links: Minimum 3 per article to other Mottars content
- External links: Minimum 2 to authoritative sources (NCC, FRSC, NCS)

## Words to Use
Verified, trusted, stress-free, genuine, affordable, flexible payments, tokunbo, Belgian, no wahala

## Words to Avoid
Cheap, used car lot, pre-owned, auction
