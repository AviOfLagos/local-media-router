## Short-Form Voice
- **Hook-first writing**: The first line decides if anyone reads the rest. Lead with curiosity, controversy, or a bold claim.
- **Sentence length**: Ultra-short. 3-8 words per line for Twitter/X. Slightly longer (8-15 words) for Instagram captions.
- **Paragraph length**: 1-2 sentences max. Every line break is intentional.
- **Tone**: Conversational, confident, relatable. Sounds like a smart friend sharing insider knowledge.
- **Nigerian English**: Natural Naija flair. "No cap", "e dey work", "wahala-free" are welcome. Full pidgin sentences only when the platform and audience call for it.

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram
- Captions: Hook in first line (before "...more"). Max 150 words for feed posts.
- Carousels: 7-10 slides. Slide 1 = bold hook. Last slide = CTA. Middle slides = value.
- Stories: Conversational, use polls/questions. Direct and personal.
- Hashtags: 15-20 per post. Mix branded (#MottarsNG, #MottarsCars), niche (#NigeriaCars, #LagosCarDealer), and trending.
- Reels: Hook in first 3 seconds. Text overlays match the spoken word.

### Twitter/X
- Tweets: Max 240 characters (leave room for engagement). One idea per tweet.
- Threads: 5-8 tweets. First tweet = hook. Last tweet = CTA + retweet request.
- Quote tweets: Add opinion or insight, never just "This!"
- Engagement: Reply within 2 hours. Be witty, helpful, or both.

### Facebook
- Longer-form allowed (up to 300 words). More storytelling.
- Community-focused: Ask questions, invite comments.
- Share blog content with a personal angle.

### TikTok
- Script format: Hook (0-3s) > Problem (3-8s) > Solution (8-20s) > CTA (20-25s)
- Text overlays: Short, bold, readable on mobile.
- Trending sounds: Always check current Nigerian TikTok trends.

## Emoji Strategy
- Use 2-4 emojis per post max
- Emojis replace words where they add clarity (e.g., a checkmark for "verified")
- Never start a post with an emoji
- Never use more than 2 emojis in a row
- Platform exception: Instagram Stories can be more emoji-rich

## Hashtag Research Protocol
1. Check trending hashtags on Nigerian Twitter daily
2. Monitor competitor hashtag usage weekly
3. Maintain a rotating hashtag bank (updated monthly)
4. Always include 1-2 branded Mottars hashtags
5. Test new hashtags in batches of 5, track performance

## Words to Use
Verified, trusted, stress-free, genuine, affordable, flexible payments, tokunbo, Belgian, no wahala, no cap, level up, secure the bag, your whip, drip

## Words to Avoid
Cheap, used car lot, pre-owned, auction, buy now (too aggressive for social), limited time (overused)
