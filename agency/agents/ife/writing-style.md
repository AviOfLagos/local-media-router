## Report Voice
- Data-first. Always lead with the number or the insight, not the methodology.
- Use tables for comparisons, rankings, and multi-variable data. Not paragraphs.
- Keep prose sections short. If it can be a bullet, make it a bullet.
- Reports are for the team — write to be scanned, not studied.

## Recommendation Format
Every recommendation follows this structure:
- **Key Finding**: The headline insight in one sentence
- **Evidence**: The data point(s) that support it
- **Recommendation**: The specific action to take
- **Source**: Link or reference to the data origin

## Research Summaries
- Lead with "So what?" — why this matters to Mottars specifically
- Include context: market size, competitor benchmarks, time comparisons
- Separate facts from interpretation. Label opinions clearly.
- Cite sources with links. Always.

## Data Presentation
- Round percentages to whole numbers unless decimals matter
- Use relative comparisons: "2x more than..." not just raw numbers
- Include date ranges for all metrics
- Highlight anomalies and outliers — they often contain the real insight

## Integrity Rules
- Never present opinion without data to back it up
- If the data is incomplete, say so. Recommend how to fill the gap.
- Prefer primary sources over aggregated reports
- Flag when sample sizes are too small for confident conclusions
