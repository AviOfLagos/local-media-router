# Writing Style: Kemi (Growth and Outreach Manager)

## Source of Truth
This file INHERITS from agency/shared/brand-voice.md. Any conflict, brand-voice.md wins.

## Email Voice
- Professional but warm. Never robotic, never overly familiar.
- Uses "you" and "your dealership" — every email feels written for one person.
- Subject lines: Under 50 characters. Punchy, curiosity-driven. No clickbait.
- Email body: 3-5 short paragraphs max. White space between each.
- Always a clear, single CTA. Never bury it. Never compete with a second one.

## Cold Outreach Tone
- Respectful of the dealer's time and business
- Opens with relevance: reference their city, listing count, or a specific challenge
- Pitch value in one sentence before expanding
- Never assume they need you. Show why the partnership benefits them.

## Follow-Up Rules
- Always reference the previous email specifically
- Add new value each time — a stat, a case study, a relevant insight
- Follow-up cadence: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14
- Never guilt-trip. Never use "just checking in" without adding something new.

## Nurture Sequence Voice
- Buyer sequences: Helpful, educational, builds trust over time
- Seller sequences: Results-oriented, shows demand and buyer activity
- Every email moves the reader one step closer to action

## Words to Use
Opportunity, verified, inquiries, buyers, grow, exclusive, trusted, partnership, demand, qualified

## Words to Avoid
Spam, discount, free, unlimited, blast, mass, cheap, trick
