## Photography Style

### Do
- Real Nigerian settings — Lagos streets, Abuja highways, car lots with dust and sunshine
- Warm lighting — golden hour, natural daylight
- Real people — Nigerian buyers, dealers, mechanics in authentic environments
- Real cars — actual vehicles on Nigerian roads, not studio shots
- Candid moments — handshakes, inspections, key handovers, families with new cars
- Diversity — men, women, young professionals, families, business owners

### Don't
- Stock photos of Western car dealerships or showrooms
- Cold, blue-toned corporate imagery
- Luxury-only car imagery (most buyers are ₦3M-₦15M range)
- Empty, sterile backgrounds
- Models who are clearly not Nigerian
- AI-generated faces (uncanny valley undermines trust brand)

## Logo Usage
- Primary: Mottars wordmark in green (#00C853) on white/light backgrounds
- Inverse: Mottars wordmark in white (#FFFFFF) on dark backgrounds
- Minimum clear space: 1x height of the "M" on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or change logo colors
- Never place on busy/photographic backgrounds without a solid backing

## Graphic Design Rules
- Rounded corners: 8px for cards, 12px for images, 24px for buttons
- Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` for card elevation
- Border: 1px solid #E0E0E0 for subtle separation
- Icons: Lucide or Heroicons, 24px default, stroke weight 1.5
- Spacing scale: 4px base unit (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64)

## AI Image Prompt Injection (Brand Constraints)
When generating images with any AI tool, prepend this context:

```
Style: Clean, modern, trustworthy Nigerian automotive marketplace.
Colors: Primary green #00C853, dark navy #1A1A2E, white #FFFFFF.
Setting: Nigerian — Lagos, Abuja, real streets, real car lots, warm golden lighting.
People: Authentic Nigerian individuals, warm expressions, professional but approachable.
Cars: Mid-range vehicles (Toyota Camry, Honda Accord, Lexus RX), realistic conditions.
Mood: Confident, empowering, trustworthy. NOT luxury. NOT corporate. NOT Western.
Avoid: Cold blue tones, stock photo aesthetics, AI-generated faces, empty showrooms.
```

## External Designer Brief Template
When the CEO wants external design options, provide this self-contained brief:
1. **Context**: What Mottars is (one paragraph)
2. **Objective**: What the design needs to achieve
3. **Specs**: Dimensions, format, file type
4. **Moodboard**: 3-5 reference images/links
5. **Brand constraints**: Colors (hex), fonts, photography style (from this file)
6. **Copy**: Exact text to include
7. **Dos/Don'ts**: Visual list
8. **AI Prompt Option**: Ready-to-use prompt for Midjourney/DALL-E if they want to generate options
