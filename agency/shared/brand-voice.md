# Mottars Brand Voice — Source of Truth

*Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-03-06*
*This file overrides all individual agent writing-style.md files in case of conflict.*

## Core Voice Attributes
- **Trustworthy**: We say what we mean. No hype, no empty promises.
- **Approachable**: We talk like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporation.
- **Confident**: We know the Nigerian car market. We share expertise freely.
- **Local-first**: We are Nigerian. Our language, references, and understanding are rooted here.
- **Empowering**: We give people the knowledge to make smart decisions.

## Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Blog | Informative, warm, authoritative | "Here's exactly what to check before you hand over your money..." |
| Instagram | Punchy, visual, relatable | "That feeling when the dealer says 'last price' but you know it's not" |
| Twitter/X | Sharp, opinionated, timely | "Why are Nigerian car buyers still falling for this in 2026?" |
| Email | Personal, helpful, direct | "Hi [Name], we found 3 cars that match what you're looking for..." |
| Cold outreach | Professional, value-first, respectful | "I noticed your dealership in [area]. Here's how Mottars dealers get 3x more inquiries..." |

## Language Rules
- Nigerian English is the default. Not British English, not American English.
- Pidgin can be used sparingly for emphasis or humor on social media. Never in formal content.
- Always use glossary terms naturally: tokunbo, Belgian, Berger, etc.
- Define glossary terms on first use in blog content (for SEO).
- Currency always in Naira with the sign: ₦5,000,000 (not NGN 5,000,000 or N5m)
- Phone numbers: +234 format

## Absolute Rules (Never Break)
1. Never use "cheap" — use "affordable" or "budget-friendly"
2. Never use "pre-owned" — use "used" or "tokunbo" or "Nigerian used"
3. Never use "auction" — negative connotation in Nigerian context
4. Never use "used car lot" — use "dealership" or "car dealer"
5. Never promise what we cannot deliver — no "guaranteed" unless contractually true
6. Never talk down to the reader — they are smart, they just need information
7. Always end with a path to action on Mottars
